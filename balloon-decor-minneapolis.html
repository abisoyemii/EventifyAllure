Good to know

Balloon décor FAQs

What kinds of events can include balloon décor?

EventifyAllure styles baby showers, birthdays, weddings and bridal events, proposals, graduations and corporate events. Share what you are celebrating and the studio can discuss styling options with you.

Do you travel to event venues?

Yes. EventifyAllure is a mobile studio based in Minneapolis and travels to venues. Service is available across Minnesota and beyond; include your venue or neighborhood in your inquiry.

What information should I include in my inquiry?

Share your event date, venue or neighborhood, event type, guest count, color or style ideas and budget range. These details help the studio begin shaping a design for your celebration.

How do I get started?

Send your event details through the booking form. The studio will follow up to start a conversation about your styling.
