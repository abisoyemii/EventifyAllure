Minneapolis service FAQ

Are you an event planner?

EventifyAllure focuses on event styling and décor, including balloon installations, backdrops, florals and tablescapes.

What occasions can you style?

Inquire about baby showers, birthdays, weddings and bridal events, proposals, graduations and corporate events.

Do you travel to event venues?

Yes. EventifyAllure is a mobile studio based in Minneapolis. Travel is available across Minnesota and beyond; include your venue location in your inquiry.

What should I include in my inquiry?

Share your event date, venue or neighborhood, event type, guest count and any color or style ideas to start the conversation.
